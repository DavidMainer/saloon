Philosophy of Anex

Mindful beauty nurtured by the spirit of Omotenashi.

In Japanese tradition, beauty is neither rushed nor superficial. It is an expression of mindfulness, immaculate cleanliness, and reverence for each client’s individuality.

At Anex Salon, our master practitioners are certified in Tokyo and Ginza techniques. We formulate our services exclusively with non-toxic, acetone-free nail gels, herbal botanicals, and high-performance scalp serums crafted in Japan.
